### 1. Scope of Work
The Provider will deliver, within the Timeline (Clause 3):
| # | Deliverable | Included |
|---|---|---|
| a | Digital product content: **one** ebook (15–20 pages) **or** 5–10 video scripts, on a topic agreed in writing | ✅ |
| b | One sales page (single page, responsive), hosted on Vercel or similar, connected to the Client's own Razorpay account | ✅ |
| c | WhatsApp automation: purchase confirmation + delivery message, 5-message nurture sequence, 1 abandoned-checkout reminder | ✅ |
| d | Launch kit: 5 Instagram post captions, 7-day story sequence, 1 countdown/launch-day plan | ✅ |
| e | 7 calendar days of post-launch technical support (bug fixes only) | ✅ |

**Not included** (quoted separately if required): video recording/editing, voice-over, paid ads management, custom domain purchase, logo/brand identity, third-party subscription fees (WhatsApp API, n8n, Interakt, domain, hosting upgrades), Meta per-message charges, translation into additional languages, more than one product, ongoing content creation, and any guarantee of sales or income.

### 2. Fees and Payment
2.1 Total fee: **₹[49,000]** ([plus GST @18% if Provider is GST-registered]).
2.2 **50% advance (₹[24,500])** on signing. Work begins on the business day after the advance is received **and** the Client has submitted the onboarding form.
2.3 **50% balance (₹[24,500])** on delivery (Day 10), before the sales page is switched to live payments / handed over.
2.4 All payments are to the Provider's business account or Razorpay link only.
2.5 The advance is non-refundable once Day 1 work has begun, except under Clause 7.3.

### 3. Timeline
3.1 Delivery within **10 business days** (Monday–Saturday, excluding national holidays) of the start date.
3.2 Client responsibilities: (a) onboarding call on Day 1, (b) brand assets and access as per the onboarding document by Day 1, (c) content feedback within **24 hours** of each draft.
3.3 Every day of Client delay in 3.2 extends the timeline by the same number of days. If the Client is unresponsive for **10 consecutive business days**, the project is considered complete as-is and the balance becomes due.

### 4. Revisions
4.1 **Two (2) rounds** of revisions are included for each of: content, sales page, and WhatsApp messages.
4.2 A "round" is one consolidated list of feedback sent in a single message.
4.3 Further rounds, or changes to the agreed topic/scope, are billed at ₹[1,500] per hour or quoted separately.

### 5. Intellectual Property
5.1 On receipt of full payment, the Client owns all **final content** created for the Client (ebook/scripts, copy, captions, messages) and their brand assets.
5.2 The Provider retains ownership of its **templates, code framework, automation workflows, prompts and know-how** ("Provider Materials"). The Client receives a perpetual, non-exclusive, non-transferable licence to use the Provider Materials as delivered, for the Client's own business.
5.3 The Provider may show the work (with results only if the Client consents in writing) in its portfolio.
5.4 The Client warrants that all material it supplies is its own or licensed, and indemnifies the Provider against claims arising from it.

### 6. Client Content and Compliance
6.1 The Client is solely responsible for the accuracy and legality of the product's claims, including health, medical, financial and income claims, and for compliance with applicable laws (including SEBI regulations for financial content, consumer protection law and ASCI advertising guidelines).
6.2 The Client is the merchant of record for all product sales, and is responsible for its own Razorpay account, refunds to its buyers, taxes and customer data.

### 7. Termination
7.1 Either party may terminate by written notice (email or WhatsApp) if the other materially breaches this Agreement and does not fix it within 5 business days.
7.2 If the Client terminates for convenience, the advance is retained and all work completed up to that date is delivered.
7.3 If the Provider fails to deliver the items in Clause 1 within the Timeline (after extensions under 3.3) and does not cure within 5 business days, the Client may terminate, and the Provider will refund the advance **less the value of deliverables already accepted**.
7.4 Clauses 5, 6, 8 and 9 survive termination.

### 8. Liability and Confidentiality
8.1 The Provider does not guarantee any sales, income, follower growth or platform approval (Razorpay, Meta/WhatsApp).
8.2 The Provider's total liability under this Agreement is limited to the fees actually paid.
8.3 Neither party is liable for delays caused by third-party platforms (Razorpay KYC, Meta verification, hosting outages) or force majeure.
8.4 Both parties will keep each other's non-public business information and login credentials confidential. Credentials are shared only via a password manager or a separate secure channel, and the Client changes them after handover.

### 9. Governing Law and Disputes
9.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of India.
9.2 The parties will first try to settle disputes amicably within 15 days.
9.3 Failing that, courts at **[City, State: the Provider's city]** have exclusive jurisdiction.

### 10. General
This is the entire agreement between the parties. Changes must be in writing (email/WhatsApp accepted). Acceptance by email/WhatsApp reply "Agreed" together with payment of the advance constitutes valid acceptance.
