# Lead Tracker: how to fill 50 real prospects in 2 evenings

## Why the handles are blank (read this)
You asked for 50 real Indian coaches with handles. I didn't make them up, for three reasons:
1. **I can't verify live Instagram follower counts or current activity from here.** A list of
   guessed handles would be full of dead accounts, wrong follower ranges, and people who already
   sell courses. You'd waste your first Monday DMing them.
2. **Big names I *could* verify (500K+ followers) are the wrong target.** They already have teams.
   Your sweet spot is 10K–100K accounts, which change every month.
3. **Spending 90 minutes picking leads yourself makes your DMs better.** You can't personalise
   line 1 of a DM (see `02-dm-scripts`) without looking at their posts anyway.

So the CSV has **50 slots pre-sorted by niche**, each with a fit reason and the exact hashtag or
search term to use. Fill them in Saturday and Sunday evening (about 90 min each) and you have a
verified list ready for Monday.

## The 3-minute qualification check (per prospect)
Open the profile and check these. A prospect needs **5 of 6** to go in the sheet.

| # | Check | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 10K–100K followers | Big enough to sell, small enough not to have a team |
| 2 | Posted in the last 7 days | Active creators actually reply |
| 3 | Comments ask questions ("plan bhejo", "how?", "price?") | Proves buying demand |
| 4 | **No** existing course/ebook link in bio (or a "coming soon") | They need you |
| 5 | Face on camera / personal brand | Trust already built with the audience |
| 6 | Engagement ≥ 2% (avg likes ÷ followers × 100, on the last 6 posts) | Real audience, not bought followers |

**Fit score:** 6/6 = 5, 5/6 = 4. Below that, don't add them.

## How to find them (free)
1. Instagram search → hashtag from the `how_to_find_search_this` column → **Recent** tab, not Top.
   Recent shows smaller, active creators.
2. Open a good match → tap the ▼ next to Follow → "Suggested accounts". This gives 10–20 similar creators instantly.
3. Email: check the bio's "Email" button and their link-in-bio page. **Skip Hunter.io for Indian
   individuals**: most use Gmail, and you should only email addresses they published themselves
   (see the DPDP note in `03-email-templates`).

## Status values (use exactly these, so the dashboard counts work)
`To research` → `Qualified` → `DM sent` → `Replied` → `Call booked` → `Proposal sent` → `Won` / `Lost` / `Not now`
