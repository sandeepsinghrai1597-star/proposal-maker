# 15-Minute Sales Call Script (Hinglish)

**How this works (plain language):** a sales call is not you "pitching". It's you **asking
questions until the prospect says their problem out loud**, then showing how you solve exactly
that. The person who talks more on the call is usually the one being convinced, so aim for them talking
60% of the time and you 40%.

The questions below follow **SPIN**, a well-known B2B sales method:
- **S**ituation: facts about them ("kitne followers?")
- **P**roblem: what's not working ("launch kyun nahi hua?")
- **I**mplication: what that problem costs them ("har mahine kitna miss ho raha hai?")
- **N**eed-payoff: they describe the better future in their own words ("agar live hota toh?")

When *they* say "yeh toh ₹50K/month ho sakta tha", that convinces them far more than you saying it.

**Before the call (5 min):** open their profile, note follower count, their top 3 posts, and the
most common question in their comments. Keep this script + the sales page open. Use headphones.

---

## 1. Opening: rapport + agenda (2 min)

> "Hi [Name]! Thank you time dene ke liye — main jaanta hoon creators ka schedule kitna packed hota hai.
> Aapki [specific reel] maine dekhi thi — [genuine 1-line compliment]."

*(Wait for them to respond, then laugh or chat for 20–30 seconds. Don't rush.)*

> "Toh agenda simple rakhte hain — pehle 5 min main aapse aapke page aur goals ke baare mein kuch sawaal poochunga, phir batata hoon main kaise help kar sakta hoon, aur end mein aap decide karna ki fit hai ya nahi. Agar fit nahi laga, main khud bol dunga. Theek hai?"

*Why this works:* telling them they can say no lowers their guard. People relax when they don't feel trapped.

## 2. Discovery: SPIN questions (5 min)

**Situation (1 min)**
1. "Aap content kab se bana rahe ho, aur abhi income kahan se aati hai — brand deals, coaching, ya kuch aur?"
2. "Ek average mahine mein Instagram se roughly kitna aata hai?"

**Problem (1.5 min)**
3. "DMs ya comments mein log kya sabse zyada poochte hain?"
4. "Kabhi apna product — ebook, course — launch karne ki try ki? Kya hua?"
   *(Most will say "start kiya tha, tech mein atak gaya" or "time nahi mila". Note their exact words. You'll repeat them later.)*

**Implication (1.5 min)**
5. "Toh jo log DMs mein 'plan bhejo' bolte hain, unka abhi kya hota hai?"
6. "Agar aaj se 6 mahine baad bhi product launch nahi hua, toh kaisa lagega?"
   *(Pause. Stay quiet for 3 seconds. Let them feel it.)*

**Need-payoff (1 min)**
7. "Agar aapke paas ek ₹[499/999] ka product hota jo automatically bikta — kitne log khareedte aapke hisaab se?"
8. "Aur woh extra income aap kis cheez mein lagate?"

*Write down the number they say in Q7. That's your anchor for the pitch.*

## 3. Pitch (5 min)

**Summarise their problem in their words (30 sec):**
> "Toh agar main sahi samjha — aapki audience already [their words: 'full plan maangti hai'], aapne [their failed attempt] try kiya tha lekin [their blocker], aur aap chahte ho ki [their goal]. Sahi hai?"

*(Wait for "haan". This "yes" matters, because people stay consistent with what they've already agreed to.)*

**Show the 4 pillars (3 min)**. Share your screen with the sales page:
> "Exactly yahi main karta hoon. 10 business days, 4 steps:
> **Day 1–3:** Aapke comments aur DMs se product ka structure banata hoon — aap approve karte ho, AI se draft, phir aapki awaaz mein final edit.
> **Day 4–6:** Yeh jaisa sales page — aapke brand mein, Razorpay ke saath. Paisa seedha aapke account mein.
> **Day 7–8:** WhatsApp automation — koi buy kare toh instant delivery, aur jo checkout chhod de, unko reminder.
> **Day 9–10:** Launch posts aur stories ready, aur launch ke baad 7 din support.
> Aapka kaam sirf teen cheezein: ek 45-min onboarding call, content approve karna, launch post daalna."

**Demo (1 min):** show your demo sales page, do a Razorpay test checkout, and show the WhatsApp message arriving on your phone. *Seeing it work is more convincing than anything you can say about it.*

**Price, using the anchor (30 sec):**
> "Agar yeh sab alag-alag hire karo — writer, designer, developer, WhatsApp setup — market mein ₹2.3L ke aaspaas jaata hai. Mera standard price ₹75,000 hai. Abhi main pehle 3 clients founder price pe le raha hoon — ₹49,000.
> Aur aapne bola tha ki [their Q7 number] log khareed sakte hain — ₹999 pe woh [calculate: e.g. 100 × 999 = ₹99,900] ho jaata hai. Matlab pehle launch mein hi recover."

**Then stop talking.** Silence after the price is normal. The first person to speak usually gives something away, so let them go first.

## 4. Objection handling (2 min)

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| **"Bahut mehenga hai"** | "Samajh sakta hoon. Ek sawaal — mehenga lag raha hai kyunki budget abhi nahi hai, ya isliye ki result pe doubt hai?" → *Budget:* "Lite package ₹29K hai — ebook + sales page + Razorpay. WhatsApp baad mein." → *Doubt:* "Isliye 50% sirf delivery ke baad hai. Agar Day 10 tak live nahi hua, aapko baaki pay nahi karna." |
| **"10 din mein ho jaayega?"** | "Haan, kyunki main har project ek tested template pe banata hoon — sirf content aur design aapka custom hota hai. Day-wise timeline written agreement mein hoti hai, aur har shaam aapko WhatsApp pe update milega." |
| **"Aapka koi past kaam?"** (trust) | Honest answer: "Aap mere pehle 3 clients mein se ho, isliye price kam hai. Lekin yeh demo dekho [show]. Aur main [SwiftSite Studio / your real web projects] pe kaam kar chuka hoon. Plus 50-50 payment — risk aapka nahi, mera hai." |
| **"Partner/spouse se poochna hai"** | "Bilkul. Main ek 1-page summary WhatsApp kar deta hoon jo aap unko dikha sakte ho. Kya hum kal 9 baje 5 min ka follow-up call rakh lein, dono saath mein?" |

## 5. Close (1 min)

> "Toh [Name], meri taraf se aap ek perfect fit ho. Aapko kya lagta hai — shuru karein?"

**If yes:**
> "Great! Main 10 minute mein WhatsApp pe teen cheezein bhejta hoon:
> 1. Service agreement — padh ke 'Agreed' reply kar dena
> 2. ₹24,500 ka Razorpay link
> 3. Onboarding form
> Payment aate hi kal Day 1 shuru. Onboarding call ke liye [day] shaam 8 baje chalega?"

**If maybe:**
> "No problem. [Day] tak main yeh founder price aur slot aapke liye hold kar raha hoon. [Day] ko 8 baje ek 5-min call karte hain final decision ke liye — theek?"
*(Book that follow-up call in Calendly **before** hanging up.)*

**If no:**
> "Bilkul theek hai, thank you honestly batane ke liye. Ek favour — kya aap kisi aur coach ko jaante ho jinko iski zaroorat ho sakti hai?"
*(Asking for a referral when someone says no often works, because they feel slightly guilty for saying no.)*
