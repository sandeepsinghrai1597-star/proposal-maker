# 30 Instagram DM Scripts (Hinglish)

## Read this first (5 min): how a DM actually works

**Rule 1: the first DM sells a reply, not the service.** Nobody pays ₹49,000 from a DM. The
first message only has to get one reply. So it's short, it's about *them*, and it ends with an
easy question.

**Rule 2: personalise one line.** Every script has a `[specific]` slot. Fill it with something real
from their last 3 posts. That one line is what separates you from spam. If you can't find one,
skip that prospect.

**Rule 3: send at most 20–25 new DMs a day** from your account. Instagram limits accounts that
mass-DM strangers. Before you DM, follow them and leave 1–2 real comments over 2 days (it warms up the thread).

**The psychology terms used below, in plain words:**
| Term | Meaning | Example in a DM |
|---|---|---|
| Curiosity | Telling half the story so they want the rest | "Ek cheez notice ki aapke comments mein…" |
| Social proof | "Others like you did it" makes it feel safe | "Ek fitness coach ke saath yahi kiya…" (only when real) |
| Loss aversion | People fear losing ₹1 more than they enjoy gaining ₹1 | "Har mahine jo buyers DMs mein aa ke chale jaate hain…" |
| Scarcity / urgency | Limited slots or a deadline | "Is mahine sirf 3 slots le raha hoon" (only if true) |
| Reciprocity | Give something free first, they feel like giving back | A free 3-point product idea for their page |

> ⚠️ Until you have a real client, do NOT use social-proof lines that claim past results.
> Scripts marked **[after 1st client]** are for later. Use the "founder offer" angle instead — being honest that you're new *and* offering a lower price is itself persuasive.

## A. Cold openers (10)

**C1 — The comment-mining opener (best one, start here)**
> Hi [Name]! Aapki [specific reel] pe comments padh raha tha — kam se kam 15 log poochh rahe the "full plan kahan milega?" 👀
> Kya aapne kabhi iska paid version banane ka socha hai — ebook ya mini-course?
> Main coaches ke liye yahi poora setup 10 din mein bana ke deta hoon. Curious tha bas.

**C2 — The free idea (reciprocity)**
> Hey [Name], aapka content [topic] pe genuinely different hai, especially [specific post].
> Maine aapke page ke liye ek digital product idea socha hai — naam, price point aur 5-chapter outline. Free mein bhejun? Koi catch nahi.

**C3 — The question opener**
> Hi [Name]! Quick sawaal — aapke [XX]K followers mein se kitne log aapse paid help maangte hain DMs mein?
> Mujhe lagta hai aapke paas ek ₹499–₹999 product ki demand already hai. Galat ho sakta hoon — aap kya sochte ho?

**C4 — The loss-aversion opener**
> [Name], aapki [specific post] ne [X] likes cross kiye — solid! 🔥
> Ek cheez bolun? Jo log aise post pe "aur batao" comment karte hain, unke liye abhi koi "buy" button nahi hai — woh demand har din waste ho rahi hai.
> Main wahi button + poora product 10 din mein setup karta hoon. Interested ho toh batata hoon kaise.

**C5 — The founder offer (honest, for first 3 clients)**
> Hi [Name]! Main ek developer hoon jo Instagram coaches ke digital products launch karta hai — ebook, sales page, Razorpay, WhatsApp automation, sab.
> Abhi apne pehle 3 clients le raha hoon founder price pe (normal price se ~35% kam), taaki solid case studies bana sakun.
> Aapka [niche] content dekh ke pehla naam aapka aaya. 10-min baat karein?

**C6 — The voice-note opener** (send a 30-sec voice note, then this text)
> Hi [Name] — upar ek chhota voice note bheja hai 🙂 Short mein: aapke [specific] wale content se ek ₹999 ka mini-course easily ban sakta hai, aur main uska poora tech + content 10 din mein kar deta hoon. Sunke batana!

**C7 — The story-reply opener** (reply to their story)
> Haha yeh [story detail] relatable tha 😄
> Btw, aap kabhi apna khud ka course/ebook laane ka plan kar rahe ho? Aapke followers definitely khareedenge — main isi kaam mein help karta hoon.

**C8 — The competitor angle**
> Hi [Name]! Aapke niche mein [other creator type] log ₹999–₹2,999 ke products bech rahe hain, aur honestly aapka content unse better hai [specific reason].
> Aapne apna product launch kyun nahi kiya ab tak — time ki dikkat hai ya tech ki?

**C9 — The "almost launched" opener** (if their bio says "course coming soon" / waitlist)
> Hey [Name], bio mein dekha "[course coming soon]" 👀 Kab tak launch ho raha hai?
> Main exactly yahi part handle karta hoon — sales page, payment, WhatsApp delivery. Agar kahin atka hua hai, 10 din mein live ho sakta hai.

**C10 — The social-proof opener [after 1st client]**
> Hi [Name]! Pichhle mahine [Client, niche] ka pehla ebook launch kiya — [real result] pehle hafte mein.
> Aapki audience unse bhi zyada engaged lagti hai [specific]. Same system aapke liye banaun? 10-min call pe explain karta hoon.

## B. Follow-ups — no reply in 48 hours (10)

Send at most **2 follow-ups** per person (48 h, then 5 days). Then stop. Pushing harder makes them block you.

**F1 — The gentle bump**
> Hey [Name], pata hai DMs mein kitna aata hai 😅 Bas upar wala message bump kar raha tha — koi pressure nahi. Product idea chahiye toh "haan" likh dena.

**F2 — Value drop**
> [Name], maine socha bina pooche hi bhej deta hoon 🙂
> Aapke page ke liye product idea: "[Product Name]" — ₹[price], [3 bullet outline]. Aapke comments mein sabse zyada yahi poocha jaata hai.
> Pasand aaye toh batana, baaki details call pe.

**F3 — The short one**
> Quick yes/no, [Name] — paid product launch karna is saal plan mein hai? 🙏

**F4 — The new-angle follow-up**
> Hi [Name], ek cheez add karna bhool gaya — product ka paisa seedha aapke Razorpay mein aata hai, main koi commission nahi leta. Sirf ek fixed setup fee. Shayad isse clarity mile.

**F5 — The calendar nudge (urgency, only if true)**
> Hey [Name], is mahine ke 3 slots mein se [2] book ho gaye. Agar aap [month] mein launch chahte ho, toh is hafte baat karna better rahega. Interested?

**F6 — The reel-reference follow-up**
> [Name], aapki nayi reel [specific] dekhi — waise hi comments phir se: "isko detail mein samjhao" 😄 Yeh exactly woh demand hai jo product mein convert hoti hai. Baat karein 10 min?

**F7 — Ask permission to close (breakup)**
> Hi [Name], lagta hai abhi sahi time nahi hai — bilkul theek hai. Main aage aur message nahi karunga 🙏
> Kabhi product launch karna ho toh yeh chat yaad rakhna. All the best!

*(F7 often gets the most replies — people respond when you take the offer away. That's loss aversion.)*

**F8 — The voice-note follow-up**
> (20-sec voice note) "Hi [Name], Sandeep here — bas ek baar personally bolna tha, aapke content mein real paisa hai. Ek baar 10 min baat kar lo, fit na lage toh main khud mana kar dunga."

**F9 — The mini case-study [after 1st client]**
> [Name], ek update share karta hoon — [Client] ka [product] launch hua, [real result]. Unke followers aapse kam hain. Aapke liye kya possible hai, yeh call pe dikhata hoon?

**F10 — The question-reversal**
> [Name], ek honest feedback chahiye 🙏 Mera message clear nahi tha ya abhi digital product aapki priority nahi hai? Aapka jawaab mujhe better karne mein help karega.

## C. Objection handling (10)

**How to handle any objection — the 3-step "Feel–Felt–Found" pattern, in plain words:**
1. **Agree with the feeling** ("Bilkul samajh sakta hoon")
2. **Normalise it** ("Zyada coaches pehle yahi sochte hain")
3. **Give one new fact or a small next step** (never argue)

### "Too expensive" / "Bahut mehenga hai"

**O1 — Break it down**
> Bilkul fair sawaal hai [Name]. Ek baar math dekhte hain: agar aapka product ₹999 ka hai, toh ₹49,000 sirf 50 sales mein recover ho jaata hai. Aapke [XX]K followers mein yeh 0.2% se bhi kam hai.
> Aur system ek baar banta hai — next 12 mahine bechte raho. Kya 50 sales aapko realistic lagti hain?

**O2 — Compare to doing it piece by piece**
> Samajh sakta hoon. Agar yeh sab alag-alag hire karo — writer, designer, developer, WhatsApp setup — toh ₹2L+ lagta hai aur 5 logon ko manage karna padta hai. Main ek price mein, ek insaan, 10 din.
> Aur aadha payment tabhi jab sab live ho jaaye. Isse risk lagbhag zero ho jaata hai na?

**O3 — Downsell (keep it, don't discount)**
> Bilkul. Ek option hai — "Lite" version: sirf ebook + sales page + Razorpay, WhatsApp automation baad mein. ₹29,000 mein. Pehle ek product launch karke dekho, phir upgrade karna. Chalega?

*(Never lower the price for the same package. Remove scope instead. Discounting tells them the first price was fake.)*

### "I'll do it myself" / "Main khud kar lunga"

**O4**
> Haan bilkul kar sakte ho, tools sab available hain! 🙂 Sirf ek cheez — aapke time ki value kya hai? Ek reel banane mein aapko kitna time lagta hai?
> Yeh setup DIY mein 40–60 ghante leta hai. Woh time content pe lagao toh aapki audience badhegi. Main tech sambhalta hoon, aap expert bane raho.

**O5**
> Great! Ek free checklist bhej deta hoon — 12 cheezein jo launch ke liye chahiye (payment gateway KYC, delivery automation, refund policy page, etc.).
> Agar beech mein kahin atak jao, mujhe ping karna. Kaam aadha bhi ho gaya ho toh baaki main finish kar dunga.

*(Giving the checklist = reciprocity. Many DIYers come back after seeing the list.)*

### "Not ready yet" / "Abhi ready nahi hoon"

**O6**
> Samajh gaya. Ek sawaal — "ready" ka matlab aapke liye kya hai? Zyada followers, content clear nahi, ya time nahi?
> Pooch isliye raha hoon kyunki zyada coaches 10K pe bhi pehla ₹1L kama lete hain — product ka size audience ke hisaab se chhota rakhte hain.

**O7**
> Koi baat nahi. Kab tak ka soch rahe ho — 1 mahina, 3 mahine? Main aapko [month] mein ek reminder bhej dunga, aur tab tak free mein ek product idea + outline bhej deta hoon taaki jab ready ho toh seedha start karein.

### "Send me more info" / "Details bhejo"

**O8**
> Zaroor! Yeh raha 1-page overview: [sales page link]
> Lekin honestly, har coach ka product alag hota hai — 10-min call pe main aapke page ke hisaab se exact idea + price point bata dunga. Kal shaam 8 ya 9 baje mein se kya better hai?

*(Always answer "send info" with info + a specific time choice. Giving two times instead of "kab free ho?" is called an "alternative close" — it makes saying yes easier.)*

### "I'll think about it" / "Sochke batata hoon"

**O9**
> Bilkul, socho. Bas ek cheez help karegi — aap kis cheez ke baare mein soch rahe ho? Price, timing, ya yeh ki result aayega ya nahi?
> Agar main abhi woh ek doubt clear kar sakun toh aapke liye decision aasaan ho jaayega.

**O10**
> Of course. Main aapke liye early-bird ₹49K price [day, date] tak hold kar deta hoon — uske baad ₹75K ho jaayega kyunki baaki slots khul jayenge.
> [Day] ko main ek baar check-in karunga. Theek hai?

*(Only use a deadline if you will actually raise the price after it. A fake deadline destroys trust the moment they notice.)*
