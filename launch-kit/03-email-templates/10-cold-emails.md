# 10 Cold Email Templates

## Before you send a single email
- **Only email addresses the creator published themselves** (Instagram "Email" button, link-in-bio,
  website "work with me" page). India's **DPDP Act 2023** governs the use of personal data, and
  emailing scraped personal Gmail addresses is risky. Hunter.io works best for creators who have their own domain.
- Send from a **separate Gmail/Zoho address on the business's (proprietor's) name**, not your official or personal email.
- Max **20 emails/day** from a new address. Plain text, no images, at most 1 link.
- Always include an opt-out line. Stop at once if someone says no.
- Subject lines: lowercase and short, like a friend would write. That gets more opens than "Exciting Opportunity!!!".

`[specific]` = one real observation from their recent content. **Never skip it.**
